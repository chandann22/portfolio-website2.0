About

Currently building, always learning.

I'm a third-year Computer Science Engineering student at Chandigarh University, working across full-stack development, relational databases and applied machine learning. I'm comfortable in Python, C++, Java and SQL, and I've been going deeper into FastAPI, LangChain / LangGraph and RAG pipelines over the last year.

I like projects with a real backend behind them — authentication, data pipelines, model inference — not just a UI on top. Right now I'm rounding out that experience with more classical algorithms and data structures, alongside coursework at Chandigarh University.
